About

I am an Informatics PhD student at Penn State, advised by Yubo Kou, and a member of the Health and Play Lab. I specialize in human-computer interaction (HCI), online communities, and digital safety. My research looks at the intersection of technology, design, and human behavior to promote safety in gaming environments.

My most recent work includes interviews about women's experiences with gendered toxicity in competitive gaming.

I earned a Bachelor's degree in Mathematics from The University of Texas at Austin. My undergraduate honors thesis for the Polymathic Scholars Honors Program was A Case Study of Glocalization: Boba Tea and Digital Food Culture, and I still have an interest in Asian American food and digital communities.
