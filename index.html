About

I am a PhD student at The Pennsylvania State University. Before research, I worked in the esports industry in a customer success role, with 20+ teams of different sizes, including TSM, Evil Geniuses, and FaZe Clan. I started playing games in elementary school and have played League of Legends for over 10 years, including two years competitively.
