To “Report A Player” on League of Legends for Gendered Toxicity

January 20, 2026

Reporting a player on League of Legends is important for competitive integrity. By selecting a few choices from a report menu, players are able to report bad actors for being toxic and other types of unsportsmanlike conduct after a game is over. The moderation system then takes over, reviewing reports and dealing punishments. As a long time League player, I’ve observed the evolution of the report page over time. In previous versions of the report menu, options like “refusing to communicate with team” and “unskilled player” were eventually removed from the report menu. While some categories remain to this day, it is noticeable when options are removed. Below is the previous report menu from 2025:

The newest version of the report menu (depicted below, as of January 19th 2026) no longer includes hate speech as a separate option, lumping it together with other types of "comms abuse." This impacts the League community because it diminishes the visibility of hate speech, such as gendered toxicity.

I argue that this is an opportunity to improve the report menu by clarifying hate speech and verbal abuse, because it maintains consistency and holds the community to a higher standard of behavior. In prior versions, having the definition of hate speech and verbal abuse allows players to distinguish the types of harm they face when playing the game. When these are all categorized together under the new term: "comms abuse," it becomes challenging to deeply understand player experiences. I see an opportunity for a report system to be designed for justice, showing that people are not able to get away with punishing teammates by being toxic, such as designs that could restore justice to victims of gendered toxicity (Koung, 2025). The insights gained from understanding pattens of hate speech and verbal abuse separately could inform developers and designers about what challenges players are actively experiencing in their game and improve on designs to mitigate gendered toxicity.

I appreciate that the newest version of the report menu separates out each option and how it relates to competitive player values: winning, fairness, and respect. This demonstrates that Riot is committed to a healthy competitive community. I hope to see that respect is further outlined for the League of Legends playerbase because it is an opportunity for Riot to set itself apart from its competitors who do not acknowledge how gendered toxicity impacts its players. In the future, I am curious to know what metrics are used to tell the story of player behavior in League of Legends and how reporting systems contribute to healthier gaming communities.
